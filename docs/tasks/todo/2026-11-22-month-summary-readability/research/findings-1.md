## Requirements summary

The Month summary needs separate Category, Amount, Share, and `vs <previous tab label>` columns, plus a prominent Spending total and secondary Debt total. Distinguish previous-month-only categories by entry presence. All saved current-month entries contribute, including future dates within that month. A short tap opens details. A long press or keyboard action enters selection with all rows checked. Preserve the subtotal and selection through drill-down and Back. Reset both when the sheet, year, or month changes. The user owns all browser and E2E checks.
